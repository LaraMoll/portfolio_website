✦ About Me

I’m a Communication & Multimedia Design student who loves exploring the digital world and turning ideas into something people can see, use and experience.

I’m interested in UX/UI, digital design, illustration and development, and I enjoy jumping between different creative disciplines. I like experimenting with new ideas and finding unexpected ways to bring them to life. My work is a mix of curiosity, creativity and a little bit of personality. I believe design can be functional while still being fun, expressive and memorable.

✦ Ambition

I’m still discovering where my journey in digital design will take me, but that’s exactly what I enjoy about it. I want to keep learning, experimenting and exploring different sides of design.

My goal is to grow into a designer who can combine visual creativity, illustration and technology to create digital experiences that feel both useful and uniquely mine. I’m not sure where it will lead yet, but I’m excited to find out.
