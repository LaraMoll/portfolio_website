Overdrive // 2.4 — Cyberpunk Identity Design Story

“Creating a complete visual identity instead of just designing a can.”

In the first year of my study, we had a project about branding (identity design), where I got the cyberpunk energy drink as my theme. As someone who is actually very interested in retro and sci-fi things, I was very excited about this project and started looking for ideas online.

I started sketching specific things that reminded me of cyberpunk. Eventually, I found out that a character was very interesting to most people, so I decided to continue with that. From there, I went for a complete cyberpunk theme and searched for fonts that gave the same feeling. I also started experimenting with different colors, shapes and graphic elements to make everything fit together.

The main goal was to create a complete visual identity instead of just designing a can. The energy drink has a 250ml can with a full 360° design, so the design continues all the way around the can. I wanted it to feel like it belonged in a cyberpunk world, with a dark futuristic look, neon-like colors and digital and technological elements.

I also created an A2 poster to expand the brand. Instead of simply putting the can on the poster, I used the same visual elements and cyberpunk style to create more of a brand world. The poster is meant to show the feeling and atmosphere of the brand rather than just being an advertisement for the drink.

I had never done any branding before, so this was quite hard for me. I had to learn how to make different design elements work together and still make the brand recognizable. Through this project, I learned how to do research online and use that research to develop my own ideas. I also learned more about typography, colors, composition and how these things can help communicate the feeling of a brand.

Cozy Farm // 1.0 — Python RPG & Character Customizer Story

“Leaving the busy city accounting life behind to cultivate a cozy, story-rich farming adventure.”

Cozy Farm was conceptualized and developed during an intensive, creative Challenge Week. As a multidisciplinary design student passionate about games, storytelling, and code, I wanted to build a heartwarming, retro-styled text RPG combined with handcrafted pixel art character avatars.

The storyline follows a burned-out city accountant seeking a quieter life. After spotting an ad from a kindly grandmother selling her countryside homestead, the player buys the property and sets off on a rural adventure filled with farming, foraging, NPC conversations, and brain-teasing quests.

🌻 The Character Customization Engine (make_your_character.py)

A core highlight of the project is the modular character creator. Instead of fixed avatars, players customize their farmer through guided terminal prompts backed by input-validation loops:

  • Skin Tones: 6 distinct tones (Pale, Fair, Medium, Olive, Naturally brown, Very dark).
  • Hair Colors: Blond, Brown, Black, and Ginger.
  • Eye Colors: Blue, Green, Brown, Black, and Grey.
  • Body Types: Muscular, Curvy, and Skinny.
  • Accessories: Straw hat, Glasses, Ribbon, Fedora, Necklace, Bracelet, and Earrings.

⚒️ Quests, Map Exploration & Riddles (cozy_farming_level_1.py)

Upon arriving at their newly named farm, the player must seek out tools. Guided by an in-game world map (spanning West Lake, North Mines, East Town, and the Southern Beach), they journey to Annie the blacksmith. Annie trades rusty starter tools in exchange for 5 fresh apples from the fruit tree forest. Along the way, players face riddle guardians with limited attempts, earning HP rewards upon solving them.

⏱️ Timers, Color Games & Team Git Workflow

We implemented time-based tension using one_minute_timer.py and an experimental color game demo. Collaboration with my project partner Somaya was managed through GitHub using feature branches, verifying each pull request before merging into the main game build.
