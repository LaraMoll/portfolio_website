Overdrive // 2.4 : Cyberpunk Identity Design Story

“Creating a complete visual identity instead of just designing a can.”

In the first year of my study, we had a project about branding (identity design), where I got the cyberpunk energy drink as my theme. As someone who is actually very interested in retro and sci-fi things, I was very excited about this project and started looking for ideas online.

I started sketching specific things that reminded me of cyberpunk. Eventually, I found out that a character was very interesting to most people, so I decided to continue with that. From there, I went for a complete cyberpunk theme and searched for fonts that gave the same feeling. I also started experimenting with different colors, shapes and graphic elements to make everything fit together.

The main goal was to create a complete visual identity instead of just designing a can. The energy drink has a 250ml can with a full 360° design, so the design continues all the way around the can. I wanted it to feel like it belonged in a cyberpunk world, with a dark futuristic look, neon-like colors and digital and technological elements.

I also created an A2 poster to expand the brand. Instead of simply putting the can on the poster, I used the same visual elements and cyberpunk style to create more of a brand world. The poster is meant to show the feeling and atmosphere of the brand rather than just being an advertisement for the drink.

I had never done any branding before, so this was quite hard for me. I had to learn how to make different design elements work together and still make the brand recognizable. Through this project, I learned how to do research online and use that research to develop my own ideas. I also learned more about typography, colors, composition and how these things can help communicate the feeling of a brand.

Cozy Farm // 1.0 : Python RPG & Character Customizer Story

“Leaving the busy city accounting life behind to cultivate a cozy, story-rich farming adventure.”

Cozy Farm was conceptualized and developed during an intensive, creative Challenge Week. As a multidisciplinary design student passionate about games, storytelling, and code, I wanted to build a heartwarming, retro-styled text RPG combined with handcrafted pixel art character avatars.

The storyline follows a burned-out city accountant seeking a quieter life. After spotting an ad from a kindly grandmother selling her countryside homestead, the player buys the property and sets off on a rural adventure filled with farming, foraging, NPC conversations, and brain-teasing quests.

🌻 The Character Customization Engine (make_your_character.py)

A core highlight of the project is the modular character creator. Instead of fixed avatars, players customize their farmer through guided terminal prompts backed by input-validation loops:

  • Skin Tones: 6 distinct tones (Pale, Fair, Medium, Olive, Naturally brown, Very dark).
  • Hair Colors: Blond, Brown, Black, and Ginger.
  • Eye Colors: Blue, Green, Brown, Black, and Grey.
  • Body Types: Muscular, Curvy, and Skinny.
  • Accessories: Straw hat, Glasses, Ribbon, Fedora, Necklace, Bracelet, and Earrings.

⚒️ Quests, Map Exploration & Riddles (cozy_farming_level_1.py)

Upon arriving at their newly named farm, the player must seek out tools. Guided by an in-game world map (spanning West Lake, North Mines, East Town, and the Southern Beach), they journey to Annie the blacksmith. Annie trades rusty starter tools in exchange for 5 fresh apples from the fruit tree forest. Along the way, players face riddle guardians with limited attempts, earning HP rewards upon solving them.

⏱️ Timers, Color Games & Team Git Workflow

We implemented time-based tension using one_minute_timer.py and an experimental color game demo. Collaboration with my project partner Somaya was managed through GitHub using feature branches, verifying each pull request before merging into the main game build.

Wanderlust & Sunshine // Boutique Travel Planner & AI Concierge

“Transforming stressful vacation logistics into sun-drenched scrapbook keepsakes and intelligent AI travel guidance.”

☀️ Concept & Design Philosophy

Developed as part of the Year 2 Communication & Multimedia Design curriculum (Creative Tech 2 • AI). The mission of Wanderlust & Sunshine is to reimagine vacation planning for seekers of all generations, with special attention to senior travelers, busy families, and first-time explorers who need accessible, calm, and dependable guidance. The platform combines warmth, sun-soaked editorial palettes (terracotta, soft sky blue, warm sand), and clear legibility to remove digital overwhelm.

📖 Tactile Scrapbook Journals & Visual Agendas (planning.html)

Instead of rigid, cold spreadsheets, trips are translated into personal scrapbook journals. The interface features realistic lined notebook paper with handwritten agenda items, washi tape accents, and angled Polaroid photo keepsakes (like surf shacks, orange orchards, and coastal roads). Each day balances planned activities with leisurely free time:

  • Day 1 : Sunlit Arrival: Scooter pickups, oceanfront check-in, Algarve sand walks, and local grilled sardine dinners under terrace stars.
  • Day 2 : Surf & Sunshine: Citrus cliff breakfasts, beginner surf lessons, shaded palm naps, and red-rock cave exploration.
  • Day 3 : Sweet Slow Living: Farmer’s market visits, fresh oranges, and scenic coastal lookouts.

🤖 Plannie: The Live AI Travel Concierge (chatbot.js)

To answer on-the-fly traveler questions, the site features Plannie, an intelligent AI travel concierge. Engineered with a refined, senior-safe persona and courteous conversational tone, Plannie provides real-time guidance covering:

  • Prices & Budgets: Low, mid, and luxury cost estimations per day in both local currencies and Euro (€).
  • Weather & Best Seasons: Real-time summer temperatures, humidity levels, and optimal travel windows.
  • Valutas (Currencies): Exact currency names, symbols, exchange rate tips, and cash vs. card advice.
  • Built-in Offline Knowledge: Pre-programmed destination intelligence for Portugal, Japan, Greece, Italy, Costa Rica, and Thailand.

♿ Senior Accessibility & Responsive Engineering

The website prioritizes WCAG-compliant high-contrast typography, generous click targets for mobile and tablet devices, clear feedback states, and structured inquiry workflows.

Juicy Snacks : Cora’s Secret Romance & The April Fools Tabloid Sensation

“1 April is the one day brands can break the rules and have fun. I turned an iconic Dutch food mascot into the talk of the country with a funny tabloid love affair.”

🥊 Agency Context: Fightclub Breda

Fightclub is a full-service creative agency in Breda where strategy, creativity, and digital come together. Their motto is ‘It’s not the size of the dog in the fight, it’s the size of the fight in the dog’, which explains the little dog in their logo. Over the years, Fightclub has grown into one of the biggest agencies in the Netherlands and Belgium, with over 100 specialists working for top brands.

🎯 The Challenge: 1-April Campaign Pitch

April 1st is the one day when brands can set normal rules aside and play with humor, self-mockery, and surprise. A strong April Fools action is more than just a quick joke: it can get free publicity and even go viral. The real challenge is making an idea that people genuinely fall for at first, without it feeling cheap or cheesy.

Fightclub asked us to pick one of their clients: Mora, Red Band, or Venco, and design an original April Fools stunt that fits the brand identity. The project was divided into two phases:

  • Phase 1 (Ideation & Pitching): Brainstorming multiple creative directions and pitching our initial ideas at the Fightclub office.
  • Phase 2 (Developing & Production): Choosing the best direction, designing all the campaign assets, and preparing a convincing final pitch.

💡 My Design Process: From Engagement Ring to Tabloid Scandal

I started by critically researching which brand I found most interesting out of the three options. I made lots of sketches for Red Band and Venco, like licorice toothpaste, licorice-flavored beer, and energy drinks. But when sketching ideas for Mora, I realized that is where my real passion was. Mora is deeply rooted in Dutch snackbar culture, and everyone recognizes their iconic brand ambassador, Cora van Mora.

My first idea was to design a deep-fried engagement ring as an April Fools prank. I sketched a ring made from a frikandel with a golden-brown bitterbal on top as a diamond, using the slogan: "Through snacks, love finds each other faster than expected". It was a fun visual joke, but as I thought about it more, I realized just a product photo would be forgotten in five seconds. I wanted a bigger story with drama and curiosity: What if Cora was secretly dating someone behind Mora's back?

📰 The Execution: Creating the "Juicy Snacks" Gossip Magazine

To bring this scandal to life in a way that felt authentic and funny, I decided to make Juicy Snacks: a full 8-page gossip magazine inspired by Dutch tabloids like Privé, Story, and Weekend.

I did research into Mora's official visual identity, extracting their specific red and orange-yellow colors, bold slab-serif fonts, and food photography style. I combined those brand rules with classic tabloid design elements: sensational starbursts, exclamation marks, paparazzi-style photos, barcodes, and dramatic quotes.

My cover started as a simpler concept layout ('Where snacks melt and hearts break', teasing that Cora was cheating with a competitor) and developed into a dramatic, tabloid-style breaking news cover with paparazzi photos and supermarket badges.

🔍 What is Inside the 8 Pages

  • Page 1 (The Cover): Big breaking headlines: "CORA CAUGHT RED-HANDED! Romantic candlelit dinner with a mystery lover", paparazzi snapshots of Cora clinking wine glasses, and gossip teasers.
  • Pages 2 and 3 (The Expose & The Rivals): Photos of her candlelit date, followed by "The Battle for Cora: Who will conquer her crispy heart?" introducing four comical suitors: Meneer Mac, Baron van Kwekboom, butcher Willem Rookworst, and chip shop owner Henk.
  • Pages 4 and 5 (Leaked Chats & Puzzles): Leaked text messages between Cora and her secret date, plus custom snack puzzles including a crossword puzzle and word search.
  • Pages 6 and 7 (The Reveal): Five shocking revelations leading up to the April 1st punchline: "Cora will never leave Mora!" The secret date was just a private taste-testing session for Mora's new extra-crispy snack recipe.
  • Page 8 (Snack Horoscope & Fryer Guide): A lighthearted back cover featuring a snack horoscope matching your zodiac sign to a Mora snack, plus a deep-fry decision flowchart.

🚀 Pitching at Fightclub

In our pitch presentation at Fightclub's office, we showed how this campaign could roll out in three steps:

  • Late March: Leaking mysterious blurry photos and clips on TikTok and Instagram to get people talking.
  • March 31st: Placing physical copies of Juicy Snacks in cafeterias and supermarket checkout lines.
  • April 1st: Dropping a funny video where Cora laughs off the rumors and reveals the true new snack.

Presenting our pitch to the creative directors at Fightclub was an awesome experience. They loved the humor, the visual research, and how well the magazine matched both Mora's identity and real Dutch gossip tabloids.
